About Turbo Intercept

Turbo Intercept Interactive is a co-venture between Ben Trinklein, solo-dev extraordinaire, and Joshua Veach, an independent artist and creative. It started as an idea to creatively blend the worlds of video games and music into unique, special experiences. The studio name comes from the Interceptor, the sleek ghost car from the 1986 cult film The Wraith.

Our first release, From Beyond: Arcade Edition, started as a question. What if the soundtrack to the game is the game? A concept album that you can listen to and play!

From Beyond's story centers around an alien invasion on Halloween night, 1986. Narrated by a snarky heavy metal radio DJ named Nasty Nick Nash, every track is a level. Every lyric becomes something you shoot down as it scrolls up the screen. A fully immersive, rock and roll arcade shoot 'em up.
