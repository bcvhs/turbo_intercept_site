ABOUT THIS GAME

FROM BEYOND: Arcade Edition is a full-length rock album you can play. The music isn't just the soundtrack. It's the entire saga.

Halloween night, 1986. Listeners of local heavy metal station 96.6 The Lazer begin reporting mysterious lights in the sky. DJ Nasty Nick Nash unknowingly narrates the last broadcast on Earth. Tune in as the new BCVHS album soundtracks an alien invasion… FROM BEYOND!

KEY FEATURES

  • An Interactive Space Rock Odyssey: Blast through a complete, 10-track concept album by the artist BCVHS, reimagined as a retro space shoot 'em up. Use your ship to destroy the song lyrics as they roll up the screen like the credits of an '80s sci-fi flick.
  • Pure Arcade Action: Focus entirely on the perfect run. With no traditional game overs, play through Story Mode to unlock Arcade Mode, allowing you to put your favorite track on repeat and maximize your score.
  • Unique Dynamic Levels: Every song features distinct enemy types, custom formations, and shifting color palettes that adapt directly to the music.
  • Peaceful Mode: If just taking in the vibes and piloting your ship is more your style, turn off the enemies. Sit back, relax, and cruise through the album stress-free.
  • Deep Customization: Trade rare space collectibles to unlock a massive vault of modifiers, including new ship designs, custom colors, and unique bullet trails.
